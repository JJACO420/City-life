# 🏙️ City Life - In-Depth Life Simulation Game

A complex life simulation game inspired by BitLife, featuring a randomly generated world with top-down pixelated graphics. Live your life, make choices, build relationships, pursue careers, and navigate through the challenges of city living!

## 🎮 Features

### Complex Life Simulation (BitLife-Level Depth)
- **Aging System**: Start at age 18 and age day by day
- **Multiple Life Stats**: Health, Energy, Hunger, Happiness
- **Character Attributes**: Intelligence, Strength, Charisma, Creativity
- **Career System**: Multiple jobs with different salary levels and requirements
  - Cashier, Office Worker, Teacher, Engineer, Doctor
  - Salary progression based on skills
  - Ask for raises based on performance
- **Education System**: Attend classes, take exams, level up education
- **Economy System**: Earn money, spend on food, activities, and luxuries

### Randomly Generated World
- **Procedurally Generated Map**: 100x100 tile world with varied terrain
- **Dynamic Buildings**: 
  - 🏠 Houses (Sleep, Watch TV, Study)
  - 🍔 Restaurants (Eat meals of varying quality)
  - 💪 Gyms (Workout, Take fitness classes)
  - 💼 Offices (Work, Ask for raises)
  - 📚 Schools (Attend classes, Take exams)
  - 🛒 Stores (Buy groceries, clothes, luxury items)
  - 🏥 Hospitals (Medical checkups, Treatments)
  - 🍺 Bars (Socialize, Have drinks)

### Living NPCs
- **50 AI-controlled NPCs** with unique:
  - Names and ages
  - Jobs and routines
  - Movement patterns
  - Personalities
- **Social Interactions**:
  - Talk to NPCs
  - Compliment people
  - Ask about work opportunities
  - Build relationships

### Top-Down Pixelated Graphics
- **Pixel Art Style**: Crisp, retro aesthetic
- **Dynamic Camera**: Follows player smoothly
- **Day/Night Cycle**: Visual overlay changes with time
- **Character Sprites**: Distinct player and NPC designs
- **Building Designs**: Unique colors and window patterns

### Life Activities
- **Career Development**: Work, earn money, get promotions
- **Education**: Study, attend school, increase intelligence
- **Health & Fitness**: Workout, eat healthy, visit doctors
- **Social Life**: Meet people, socialize at bars, volunteer
- **Mental Health**: Go to therapy, take vacations
- **Hobbies**: Start creative pursuits (painting, music, sports, etc.)
- **Shopping**: Buy food, clothes, and luxury items

### Random Events
- Find money on the street
- Get surprise bonuses
- Experience health issues
- Have inspiring conversations
- And many more random life occurrences!

## 🎯 How to Play

### Controls
- **WASD / Arrow Keys**: Move your character around the city
- **SPACE**: Interact with nearby NPCs or buildings
- **E**: Open actions menu (view stats, change jobs, relationships, etc.)
- **R**: Quick rest to restore some energy
- **ESC**: Close menus

### Getting Started
1. Open `index.html` in a web browser
2. Your character spawns in a randomly generated city
3. Walk around using WASD or arrow keys
4. Approach buildings and press SPACE to interact
5. Talk to NPCs to build relationships
6. Work to earn money
7. Manage your stats (health, hunger, energy, happiness)
8. Make life decisions through the actions menu (E key)

### Gameplay Tips
- **Keep your hunger above 20%** to avoid health loss
- **Rest regularly** to maintain energy
- **Work when energy is high** for better earnings
- **Study to increase intelligence** for better job opportunities
- **Exercise to increase strength** and health
- **Socialize to build charisma** and happiness
- **Save money** for important purchases like healthcare and education
- **Watch for random events** that can help or challenge you

## 🎨 Game Features in Detail

### Stats Management
- **Health**: Affected by eating, sleeping, and random events
- **Energy**: Depletes with activities, restored by rest and sleep
- **Hunger**: Decreases over time, restore by eating
- **Happiness**: Influenced by all activities and social interactions

### Character Attributes
- **Intelligence**: Improves through study and education
- **Strength**: Increases with exercise and physical activities
- **Charisma**: Builds through social interactions
- **Creativity**: Develops through hobbies and creative pursuits

### Career Progression
Start as unemployed and work your way up:
1. **Cashier** ($50/day) - Requires 10 Intelligence
2. **Office Worker** ($100/day) - Requires 30 Intelligence
3. **Teacher** ($150/day) - Requires 60 Intelligence
4. **Engineer** ($250/day) - Requires 80 Intelligence
5. **Doctor** ($400/day) - Requires 90 Intelligence

### Time System
- Time progresses automatically
- 1 in-game hour = 10 real seconds
- Day/night cycle with visual changes
- Stats decrease over time (hunger, energy)
- Age increases daily

## 🛠️ Technical Details

### Built With
- **HTML5 Canvas** for rendering
- **Vanilla JavaScript** for game logic
- **CSS3** for UI styling
- **Procedural Generation** for world and NPCs

### Architecture
- **Game Loop**: 60 FPS rendering and update cycle
- **Entity System**: Player, NPCs, and Buildings as objects
- **World Generation**: Random terrain and building placement
- **State Management**: Real-time stat tracking and updates
- **Event System**: Random life events with consequences
